Lemon Law Practice in New York, NY

We work from 45 Broadway in New York, NY, and hear from vehicle owners across New York State, including the five boroughs, Long Island, the Hudson Valley, and upstate cities. A repair order, warranty booklet, or correspondence can be reviewed from anywhere in the state.

We look at recurring engine, transmission, brake, electrical, steering, battery, and dashboard-warning problems recorded in repair orders during warranty repairs. We do not take on collisions, personal-injury claims, or routine maintenance disputes without a warranty-defect record.

During a first call, we ask for the vehicle identification number, purchase or lease agreement, warranty booklet, repair orders, invoices, and manufacturer correspondence. We review the dates, mileage entries, complaint notes, and repair attempts before discussing possible next steps.
